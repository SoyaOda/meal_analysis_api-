USDA FNDDS DATABASE STRATEGY

The USDA FoodData Central database contains TWO types of entries:

1. COMPOSED ENTRIES (preferred for accuracy):
   - "Pasta with tomato-based sauce, home recipe" (106 kcal/100g)
   - "Caesar salad, with romaine, no dressing" (77 kcal/100g)
   - "Tea, iced, brewed, black, pre-sweetened with sugar" (32 kcal/100g)

2. SIMPLE INGREDIENT ENTRIES:
   - "Pasta, cooked" (157 kcal/100g) ← 50% higher than composed!
   - "Cheese, parmesan, grated" (420 kcal/100g)

CRITICAL RULE: ALWAYS prefer composed entries when the dish matches.
- A plate of pasta with red sauce → "Pasta with tomato-based sauce, home recipe"
- NOT → "Pasta, cooked" + "Sauce, tomato" separately

Only use extras for clearly visible toppings NOT part of the main dish.
