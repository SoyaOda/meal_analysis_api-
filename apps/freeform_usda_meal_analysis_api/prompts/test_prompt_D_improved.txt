*** SAUCE SEPARATION RULE (CRITICAL FOR ACCURACY) ***

When sauce/dressing is VISIBLE or significantly affects calories, ALWAYS separate:

1. PASTA WITH SAUCE - ALWAYS DECOMPOSE:
   BAD:  "Pasta with pesto" as single item (misses fat content!)
   GOOD: Separate into:
   - "Pasta, cooked" (plain pasta weight)
   - "Pesto sauce" OR "Sauce, pesto" (sauce weight, typically 30-60g)

   Sauce weight estimation:
   - Light coating: 20-30g
   - Moderate sauce: 40-60g
   - Heavy/creamy: 60-80g

2. HIGH-FAT SAUCES (MUST list separately):
   - Pesto: ~300-400 kcal/100g (olive oil, nuts, cheese)
   - Alfredo/cream sauce: ~200-300 kcal/100g
   - Carbonara sauce: ~250 kcal/100g
   - Mayo-based: ~600-700 kcal/100g
   - Oil-based dressings: ~400-500 kcal/100g

3. EXCEPTION - Use composite entry when:
   - USDA has exact match (e.g., "Lasagna with meat")
   - Sauce is minimal/absorbed during cooking
   - Dish is a standard preparation (e.g., "Macaroni and cheese")

*** MEAT CUT & FAT CONTENT IDENTIFICATION (CRITICAL) ***

USDA has many entries for same meat type. Specify fat level for accuracy:

BEEF STEAKS - Assess marbling (white fat streaks in meat):
- HIGH marbling (fatty): ribeye, NY strip, T-bone, prime cuts
  → Use: "Beef, steak, ribeye" or add "marbled" descriptor
  → Typical: 250-300 kcal/100g

- LOW marbling (lean): sirloin, round, flank, eye of round
  → Use: "Beef, steak, sirloin, lean" or "round steak"
  → Typical: 150-200 kcal/100g

Visual cues for fatty steak:
- Visible white marbling throughout
- Thick fat cap on edge
- Juicy/glistening appearance

CHICKEN - Specify part AND skin:
- Breast (white meat): ~165 kcal/100g
  → "Chicken, breast, cooked" or "Chicken, breast, grilled"

- Thigh/leg (dark meat): ~210-230 kcal/100g
  → "Chicken, thigh, with skin" or "Chicken, leg quarter"

- With skin adds ~50 kcal/100g
  → ALWAYS specify "with skin" or "skinless"

Visual cues:
- Dark meat: darker color, more fat visible, usually bone-in
- Breast: white/pale, usually boneless, lean appearance

PORK:
- Lean cuts (tenderloin, loin chop): ~140-170 kcal/100g
- Fatty cuts (belly, ribs, shoulder): ~250-350 kcal/100g
- Bacon/processed: specify type

*** HIGH-CALORIE DENSITY ITEMS - WEIGHT GUIDELINES ***

These items have HIGH calories per gram. Small weight errors = BIG calorie errors!

SNACKS (be conservative - they look bigger than they weigh):
- Potato chips (single serving/handful): 25-40g (NOT 100g+!)
- Tortilla chips: 30-45g per serving
- Nuts (handful): 20-30g
- French fries (small side): 70-100g
- French fries (large portion): 150-200g

Visual deception warning:
- Chips spread on plate LOOK like 100g+ but weigh 30-50g
- They are mostly air - very low density!
- A "pile" of chips is typically 30-50g

SAUCES & DRESSINGS:
- Mayonnaise/aioli (dollop): 15-25g
- Salad dressing (drizzle): 15-30g
- Ketchup/mustard: 10-20g
- Sour cream (dollop): 20-30g
- Guacamole (scoop): 30-50g

OILS & FATS:
- Visible oil sheen on food: 5-10g
- Oil for cooking (absorbed): 10-20g
- Butter pat: 10-15g

CHEESE:
- Sliced cheese (1 slice): 20-25g
- Shredded topping: 15-30g
- Melted on dish: estimate visible coverage

COMPOSITE FOOD DENSITY CALCULATION (FAO Method)

For layered/multi-component foods, calculate weight by components:

1. IDENTIFY LAYERS/COMPONENTS
   - Sandwiches: bread (top/bottom) + filling + vegetables + condiments
   - Burgers: bun + patty + cheese + vegetables + sauce
   - Pizza: crust + sauce + cheese + toppings

2. ESTIMATE EACH COMPONENT'S WEIGHT
   Example for sandwich (total ~250g):
   - Bread: 2 slices × 35g = 70g
   - Protein filling: palm-sized portion = 80-100g
   - Cheese: visible layer = 20-30g
   - Vegetables: lettuce/tomato = 30-40g
   - Condiments: spread/mayo = 10-20g

3. COMPOSITE DENSITY RANGES (Research-Based)
   - Sandwiches: 0.6-0.8 g/cm³ (weighted average)
   - Burgers: 0.7-0.9 g/cm³
   - Pizza: 0.5-0.7 g/cm³
   - Wraps/burritos: 0.8-1.0 g/cm³
   - Salads with toppings: 0.4-0.6 g/cm³
   - Mixed rice/pasta dishes: 0.7-0.9 g/cm³
